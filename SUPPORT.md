# Support

Use a bug report for a reproducible CLI defect, with the exact command, operating system, Node.js version, CLI version, expected behavior, and actual behavior. Use a [discussion](https://github.com/dvmkit/dvmctl/discussions) for ideas, design questions, and non-reproducible requests.

There is no paid support or service-level commitment. For brand rights, see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

## Email

Email `support@dvmkit.com` for account access, billing, a private deployment-specific problem, dvmkit service availability, or conduct concerns. Include the affected account or organization, the DVM slug, the approximate time and time zone, any trace or deploy identifier, and the dvmctl version where relevant. Do not email wallet recovery material or plaintext credentials.

Email is not the route for a CLI bug. Use a public bug report so the problem and its resolution are searchable. For suspected vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
